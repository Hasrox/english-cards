English Cards

The installer is English-Cards.apk.

Put that file on the phone and tap it.
Android will say it is not from the Play Store. Allow the install.
If English Cards is already on the phone, this file updates that same app.
Do not uninstall the old one first. Uninstalling deletes the words saved on the phone.
The child's deck, pictures, and progress stay on the phone.

The other files are the project, for editing on a computer.
Screens are in src.
Group pictures are in public/ui.
Changing those files does not change the phone until a new installer is made.
The android folder is the phone shell. It uses the same app name every time, so the next installer is an update, not a second app.
